Privacy

Privacy policy

Effective 27 November 2026

This policy covers the game Topplonium for Android and the website amazedsaints.com, both made by Amazedsaints. The short version: the game collects nothing about you, shows no ads and has no tracking.

Topplonium (the game)

  • No personal data. The game does not ask for, collect, store or share any personal information. It has no account, no sign-in and no sign-up.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The game contains no advertising, analytics or tracking software, and it does not connect to any server of ours.
  • Your progress stays on your phone. Levels, stars, coins and settings are saved in a file on your device. Android's own backup, if you have it turned on, may copy that one file to your Google account so your progress comes back on a new phone. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy; we never see it.
  • One optional purchase. The game offers a single one-time purchase that unlocks every level. The payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your card or payment details; Google tells the game only whether the purchase was made.
  • Permissions. The game does not ask for access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or files.

amazedsaints.com (this website)

  • The site is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) to deliver the pages and protect the site from abuse.
  • We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits. It does not use cookies, does not fingerprint you and does not track you across other sites; it gives us totals such as page views and countries.
  • The site sets no cookies. It remembers, for the current browser tab only, that you have already seen the opening titles.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to fetch them.
  • If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

Children

Topplonium is suitable for all ages. Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
